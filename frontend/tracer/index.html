What the numbers mean

Sources before rankings

100 paired EN–VI scenarios: 80 answerable and 20 controls. Evidence consists of exact spans in frozen official AWS documents. A fact may accept verified alternative spans; all required facts must be covered.

Recall@5 averages the fraction of required evidence groups found per answerable question. CompleteEvidence@5 requires every group. ContextComplete checks the final packed context, limited to 8 chunks and 4,096 proxy tokens. Neither measures generated answer correctness.

All 80 answerable scenarios have an oracle evidence set of at most 5 chunks for the current spans. This proves feasibility, not semantic correctness of labels.

Exposure and limitations

A bounded review of 170 prior local inputs identified 34 related cases. Related intent families stay together: 41 development / 59 reserved evaluation, 72 assigned groups. All questions were seen by the AI annotation author; this is not an independent holdout. Human-approved labels: 0.

Graph uses 17 assertions from 9 of the 316 source documents. No usable packed relation means fallback to Hybrid. In the original English core batch, three requests used a relation, including two controls; this was not three correct answers. Inspect the selected language's Graph use column and cases for the current results.

Failed answerable attempts remain in the denominator with zero coverage. Missing methods remain null. Alternatives and translations still need independent review. No confidence or significance claim is made.

Replay identity

Recorded local CPU retrieval only. Shared embeddings are reused; local timings are not AWS answer latency. Opening, filtering or exporting these artifacts does not call a model. Historical 20-document experiments remain in Retrieval Lab and are not collection-316 scores.
